VRunner

VR Runner · Unity / C#

A VR runner built for head-mounted displays. The main challenge was making fast movement feel good in VR without causing discomfort — the whole movement system was built around that. Spatial audio reacts to speed and environment, and physics interactions make obstacles feel tangible rather than just visual.

Technical Breakdown

  • VR Locomotion — Built a custom movement system from scratch to keep speed high without causing motion sickness — involved a lot of camera decoupling and tweaking physics response per frame.
  • Spatial Audio — 3D positional audio that changes based on player speed and surroundings. Getting this to feel right in VR took careful tuning — too much and it's overwhelming, too little and it's flat.
  • Performance for HMDs — Hitting 90fps on standalone VR hardware meant being aggressive with draw call batching, LOD, and async asset loading. VR doesn't forgive frame drops the way flat games do.
