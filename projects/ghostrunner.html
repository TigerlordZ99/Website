Ghost
Runner

React Native (Expo) · GPS · Spatial Audio

An AR fitness app that paces you in real time against a "Digital Ghost" — a GPS recording of a previous run. Built in React Native with Expo, it uses geospatial logic and a Kalman Filter to smooth noisy GPS data so the ghost position stays accurate even when your phone signal drops. Spatial audio gives you real-time feedback on whether you're ahead or behind. Won "Wildest Idea" at CruzHacks 2026 out of 89 teams.

Technical Breakdown

  • Kalman Filter — Raw GPS data is noisy — especially at running speeds. Implemented a Kalman Filter to smooth out positional jitter and keep the ghost position accurate in real time without introducing lag.
  • Spatial Audio Feedback — Low-latency spatial audio tells you whether you're ahead or behind the ghost without looking at your phone. The idea was to keep your eyes on the road, not the screen.
  • React Native + Expo — Built cross-platform in React Native with Expo so it runs on both iOS and Android. Geospatial logic handles coordinate math, distance calculations, and ghost playback from stored GPS traces.
