Beat
Brawler

Rhythm Fighter · Unity / C#

A fighting game where timing your attacks to the beat actually matters. Hit on-beat and you get bonus damage and combo effects — miss and you're left open. Each AI opponent runs on a unique rhythm pattern pulled from the track, so figuring them out is part of the game. Built in Unity with a custom HFSM/Behavior Tree AI and Python-extracted spectral data to adjust aggression based on the music's energy.

Technical Breakdown

  • Beat-Sync Combat — Custom BPM tracker with ±50ms timing windows. Hitting on-beat triggers combo multipliers and VFX tied directly to the music — missing costs you more than just damage.
  • HFSM + Behavior Tree AI — Each opponent uses a Hierarchical Finite State Machine combined with a Behavior Tree for state decisions. Their attack patterns are tied to the track so every fight plays differently.
  • Spectral Data Pipeline — Python scripts extract energy levels from the audio file and feed them into the AI — enemies get more aggressive during high-energy sections of the song.
